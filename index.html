About

The mechanics on screen and the systems behind them

I'm a final-year Computer Engineering student and game programmer. I'm interested in system architecture on both sides of game development: the gameplay and the engine room. I write code comfortably in both the Unity and Unreal Engine ecosystems and use the strengths of each in my projects.

When I build, I focus not only on the mechanics on screen but also on the subsystems running behind the game:

  • Gameplay & PhysicsDynamic mechanics and realistic vehicle physics systems.
  • Performance & OptimizationHigh-performance object pooling and memory/resource management architectures.
  • Tools & AutomationOpenCV-based Python automation tools that process the screen and data from outside the game.

I love turning ideas into game systems that work, perform well and scale. Take a look at my projects below, or get in touch to talk about the systems I build.
